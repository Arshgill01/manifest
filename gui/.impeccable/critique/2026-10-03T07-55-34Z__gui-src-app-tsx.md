## Anti-Patterns Verdict
LLM: does not read as AI slop. It deliberately borrows the desktop-agent-app grammar (sidebar sessions, composer, inspector) and re-purposes it: the composer is the replay transport, sessions are runs. Typography split (system sans / mono / serif-italic model voice) carries meaning, not decoration.
Detector (CLI, 1): `side-tab` at app.css:556 (scrubber segment `border-right: 2px` surface gap): false positive in intent, but cheap to remove.
Detector (browser, 10): 8× tiny-text (11.5px metadata: split-note, net-why, panel-sub, f-detail; 10.7px nested `.mono`), 1× line-length (~105ch routine summaries), 1× text-overflow (scrub label for a zero-width round in a resumed run).

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Run switch opens on an empty trace. Why: the first thing a judge sees after a click is "No task yet / 0 of 2,600". Fix: gate the "show whole story" effect on the run whose load just finished, not on stale `ready`. Command: harden.
- [P1] The Warden block is buried. Why: demo beat 4 hinges on it, but it is only reachable by clicking ledgerly-02 R1; the scrubber marker is not clickable. Fix: list blocks in the round's growth card with a "view" action that pins the trace; make scrubber markers clickable. Command: clarify.
- [P2] The legend disappears after the first event. Why: a judge 3 m away needs "ƒ = code, 4B = student" while the trace is moving. Fix: a persistent compact key in the trace header. Command: clarify.
- [P2] Tiny metadata text (≤11.5px, nested mono 10.7px). Why: unreadable on a projector. Fix: xs → 12px; stop em-scaling `.mono` inside small text. Command: typeset.
- [P3] Results edge states: empty bill wraps per word; bill orphaned on its own row; scrub labels overflow zero-width rounds; 105-char routine summaries. Command: polish.

## Persona Red Flags
Judge (3 m, 10 s glance): reads ONLINE/OFFLINE and the red→green board instantly; cannot tell what amber vs steel means once the legend is gone; never sees the Warden block unless the presenter hunts for it.
Presenter (power user): good keys (space, ←/→, 1–5, L, G), but none are shown anywhere; a misclick on a run resets to event 0 mid-pitch.
First-time developer opening their own run: drop-to-open works; "ask-student · no routine applied" is clear; "R0 baseline" is fine; `seed`/`grown` chips are unexplained.

## Minor Observations
- Baseline traces are 12 near-identical amber pills; consider collapsing repeated tool sequences.
- Inspector header copy wraps to two lines at 1280px.
- Model rows' italic serif is the one non-native face; it earns its place as the "model voice".

## Questions to Consider
- What if the scrubber's event markers were the primary way to navigate to the story beats (proposal, Warden, gate)?
- Should the board pips be bigger, since red→green is the single most important visual?
